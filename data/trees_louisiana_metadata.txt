filename: trees_louisiana.csv
data source: US Department of Agriculture, Forest Service. Nationwide Forest Inventory (NFI).

This dataset contains data on trees of three species from the U.S. nationwide forest inventory.
Data was collected in Louisiana from 2023 to 2025. 

# Column Description #
Year: year of measurement
Plot: plot of the measured tree
Diameter: tree diameter [inches]
Height: tree height [feet]
Species: species name
Status: Life status of tree; 0 - no info; 1 - alive; 2 - dead
